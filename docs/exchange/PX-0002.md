# PX-0002: FAMILY.md 1.1 to 1.2, amended in its interim home

FAMILY.md's canonical home is cve-digest and it has not landed there yet
(PX-0001 item 5). Until it does, the copy in Pirx is the only one, and its
header says amendments are made there as entries like this one. The point of
the entry is that the landing copies a known version, with a record of what
changed since the last one anyone on the cve-digest side read.

## What changed in 1.2

- **Header.** Records cve-digest as the canonical home, as cve-digest's own
  estate-layer brief (0.7.14.4) already decided, and states that Pirx holds
  the only and authoritative copy until the landing.
- **Section 3.5, vendoring rule, narrowed.** Only FAMILY.md is shared across
  the family. Workflow documents stay repository-scoped - cve-digest
  `WORKFLOW.md`, Pirx `docs/MERGE-PROCEDURE.md` - because a vendored process
  document is a second source of truth and stale by construction. A rule
  worth adopting travels as a `convention-amendment` and is restated in the
  receiving document's own words.
- **Section 3.2, `convention-amendment` row.** Matches the narrowed rule: it
  carries changes to FAMILY.md and process rules one workflow document adopts
  from the other, not propagation of a vendored WORKFLOW.md.
- **P13 provenance.** No longer pins a WORKFLOW.md version it would have to
  keep current.

## What is asked of cve-digest

At the landing: take `docs/FAMILY.md` verbatim at whatever version Pirx's copy
then declares (1.2 or later, each later one with its own entry), create
`docs/exchange/`, and record mirrors for PX-0001 and this entry.

## What this entry does not do

- It asks for no change to `WORKFLOW.md`.
- It does not move the landing earlier. The 0.8.0.0 sequencing is cve-digest's
  and this entry does not argue with it.
